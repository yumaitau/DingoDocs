# Product

## Register

product

## Users

DingoDocs serves penetration testing consultancies, independent testers, internal security teams, red and purple teams, application and cloud security teams, and government assurance teams. Users work with sensitive assessment data under delivery pressure and need to move quickly between scope, evidence, findings, reviews, reports, and remediation without losing context or weakening access controls.

## Product Purpose

DingoDocs is a self-hosted engagement management and reporting platform for the complete professional security-assessment lifecycle. It replaces fragmented documents, generic ticketing tools, and opaque proprietary platforms with one auditable system for planning work, collecting evidence, reviewing findings, publishing reports, collaborating with clients, and verifying remediation. Success means a team can deliver a defensible assessment from scope approval to final report while retaining full ownership of its data.

## Brand Personality

Precise, trustworthy, and calm. The product should feel capable and deliberate without looking severe, theatrical, or bureaucratic. Its voice is concise, direct, and professionally reassuring.

## Anti-references

Avoid legacy enterprise layouts, excessive dashboards, dense modal workflows, unnecessary page reloads, overly complicated navigation, and generic admin-template styling. Do not imitate cybersecurity stereotypes such as neon-on-black palettes, terminal decoration, glowing threat maps, or fear-driven copy. Avoid ornamental cards, gratuitous glass effects, and visual noise.

## Design Principles

1. **Keep the engagement in context.** Scope, evidence, findings, reviews, and reporting should feel like parts of one continuous workspace.
2. **Make security state legible.** Access, classification, approval, and publication state must be explicit at the point of action.
3. **Optimise for expert flow.** Keyboard access, persistent filters, saved views, sensible defaults, and fast transitions should reward repeated professional use.
4. **Earn trust through restraint.** Use clear hierarchy, precise language, and visible auditability instead of decoration or claims.
5. **Progressively disclose complexity.** Common work stays direct while advanced controls remain discoverable and close at hand.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. All core workflows must support keyboard-only operation, visible focus, screen readers, zoom and reflow, reduced motion, and color-independent status communication. Responsive layouts must work from mobile devices through wide desktop workstations. The global command palette must open with both Command+K and Control+K.
